Our Story

A Legacy of Flavour
& Passion

Born from a simple dream — to serve food that touches the soul — Saffron Spoon has evolved into a culinary landmark. Every dish is a love letter written in spice, flame, and tradition, crafted with ingredients sourced from local farms and global spice markets.

Our founder, Chef Arjun Malhotra, spent a decade training across kitchens in Paris, Tokyo, and Istanbul before returning home to create a space where Indian heritage meets contemporary finesse.

Chef Arjun Malhotra Founder & Executive Chef
